How We Work

A Clear Path From Idea to Everyday Use.

  1. 01

    Understand and Prioritize

    We learn about your goals and how your team works. Together, we identify opportunities and decide where to start based on impact, feasibility, and investment.

  2. 02

    Build and Test

    We design the solution, agree on scope, and define how to evaluate success. We build an initial version, test it with users, and refine it.

  3. 03

    Implement and Adopt

    We introduce the solution into your operations and help your team make it part of their work. We measure results and agree on the support and improvements it needs.

About

Rooted in Colombia. Focused on What’s Possible.

WAIACAN takes its name from the guayacán, a tree known for its vibrant yellow blossoms and deep roots in Colombia.

The name also evokes a question: what can you achieve when business knowledge, creativity, and technology come together?

WAIACAN was founded by Santiago Estrada, an administrative engineer with a master’s degree in entrepreneurship from Rotterdam School of Management and more than 15 years of experience in marketing, growth, and technology.

His background combines founding businesses and leading teams with developing business tools, data systems, and automations.

Based in Colombia, we bring an international perspective and a belief that practical technology should be accessible to more businesses.
