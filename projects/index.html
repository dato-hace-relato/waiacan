SWEAT440 · Data & Business Intelligence

Business Intelligence for a Franchise Network.

The Challenge

Bring information from multiple platforms together and give corporate teams and franchisees access to the data relevant to their responsibilities.

The Solution

A reporting portal combining Google Ads, Meta Ads, Google Analytics, Google Maps, Google Search Console, and organic Instagram data.

User management and access permissions allow each profile to view the information relevant to their role.

The Result

More than 45 franchises served through a single business intelligence portal.

A centralized view of performance with tailored access for different teams and franchisees.

SWEAT440 · Web Development & Automation

A Faster Website With Lower Operating Costs.

The Challenge

Reduce the cost and effort of maintaining a website with more than 500 pages, improve performance, and simplify updates across a growing studio network.

The Solution

Migration from WordPress to a static HTML architecture, supported by automated workflows for updating offers, creating landing pages, and adding new studios.

The Result

  • PageSpeed scores increased from 32–37 to 93–99 out of 100.
  • Hosting costs reduced by more than 90%.
  • Development and maintenance costs reduced by more than 90%.
  • Maintenance time reduced by more than 50%.
  • More than 500 pages migrated.

A faster experience for visitors and a platform the team can manage more efficiently.

SWEAT440 · Automation & Growth Technology

Greater Capacity to Manage Advertising.

The Challenge

Create and manage campaigns across a growing studio network while maintaining oversight of campaign settings and ad performance.

The Solution

Automated campaign creation and management, supported by dashboards that flag underperforming ads and quality checks that identify potential configuration errors.

The Result

Campaign creation time fell from two weeks to 1.5 days while serving 30% more studios.

The operation enables one person to manage more than 60 advertising accounts, freeing capacity equivalent to an additional full-time role.

SWEAT440 · AI for Operations

Brand Monitoring Across 45 Instagram Accounts.

The Challenge

Review content across a network of Instagram accounts to identify potential violations of brand standards.

The Solution

A tool that audits posts and flags content requiring review, helping the team oversee the network.

The Result

The team can focus on flagged posts and spend less time inspecting every account manually.

Dato Hace Relato · Independent Project · Analytics & Content Tools

Turning Sports Data Into Visual Stories.

The Challenge

Transform large volumes of sports statistics into visual content that explains matches, compares teams, and tells stories through data.

The Solution

Tools for extracting and analyzing advanced statistics from matches and competitions, paired with a visualization engine that generates animated charts.

The system supports bar charts, pie charts, timelines, cohort charts, standings tables, and radar charts. A visual match playback was also developed to tell the story of World Cup knockout matches.

The Result

An in-house capability to turn sports data into analysis and audiovisual content.

The tools have been used in published Dato Hace Relato content, connecting research, analysis, and visual production.
